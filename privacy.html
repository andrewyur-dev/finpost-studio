Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

FinPost Studio is a local-first application. It does not ask for or store your TikTok, Google, or Instagram password.

Information processed

FinPost Studio processes video files, episode information, platform descriptions, selected account profiles, disclosures, and publishing status supplied by the creator. When a creator connects a platform account, the platform may provide an account identifier, username, access token, refresh token, and authorized scopes.

How information is used

Information is used only to validate the selected video, prepare platform-specific publishing metadata, verify the intended destination account, perform creator-confirmed publishing, and show the resulting status.

Local storage and security

Video files, account configuration, publishing history, and encrypted OAuth tokens are stored on the creator’s own device. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Client secrets and tokens are not intentionally transmitted to this public website.

Information shared with platforms

When the creator explicitly confirms a live publish, FinPost Studio sends the selected video, description, applicable disclosure, publishing settings, and authorization token to the selected platform’s official API. Each platform processes that information under its own privacy policy.

TikTok data

For TikTok, FinPost Studio uses authorized profile information to verify the creator identity and uses the Content Posting API only to publish content selected and confirmed by that creator. TikTok authorization can be revoked through TikTok account settings.

Retention and deletion

Creators control the local application data. Local uploads, history, configuration, and encrypted tokens can be removed from the device. Removing a published post from a social platform must be done through that platform.

Children

FinPost Studio is intended for adult creators and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a request in the FinPost Studio support repository. Do not include passwords, tokens, or private financial information.
